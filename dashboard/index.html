Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued: the implied value is this radar’s own claim, never make.fun’s.

How the value is worked out

implied = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity × chains

  • Multiple: the median mcap / TVL of the protocols in the same category that already have a token trading (at least 4 comparables), otherwise the snapshot-wide median; clamped to 0.02–1.5.
  • Growth: 1 + 0.5 × clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) / 100.
  • Maturity: 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age, 4 years) / 4 years; age unknown counts as 1 year.
  • Chains: 1 + 0.03 per extra chain, up to 5.

A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama’s record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol for it. That is the record’s word, not proof: a protocol on the list may still have a token its record does not carry. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart in the Live section below, at the market’s own value.
